Lesson 1: Spanish Verbs

In Spanish, verbs in their basic form (called the infinitive) always end in -AR, -ER, or -IR. This is the equivalent of adding "to" before a verb in English (like "to eat" or "to sleep").

Essential Verbs to Learn:

Note: Memorize these infinitives first. They are the building blocks for creating sentences later!
  • Comer: to eat
  • Dormir: to sleep
  • Jugar: to play
  • Caminar: to walk
  • Hablar: to talk
  • Pensar: to think
  • Correr: to run
  • Cantar: to sing
  • Esperar: to wait
  • Dibujar: to draw
  • Aprender: to learn
