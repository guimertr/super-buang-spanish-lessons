Lesson 3: Let's...

When you want to suggest doing an activity together with someone, English uses the phrase "Let's...". In Spanish, we use the structure "Vamos a...".

The Formula

Vamos + a + [Verb in Infinitive]

Examples:

  • Let's eat = Vamos a comer
  • Let's sleep = Vamos a dormir
  • Let's play together = Vamos a jugar juntos

We can also add time markers at the end of the sentence to be more specific:

  • ...por la mañana (in the morning)
  • ...por la tarde (in the afternoon)
  • ...por la noche (at night)
