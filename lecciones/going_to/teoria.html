Lesson 5: The 'Going to' Future

To talk about plans in the near future, English uses the "Subject + be + going to + Verb" structure (e.g., I am going to eat). Spanish uses a very similar, but simpler, structure.

The Formula

Ir (conjugated) + a + [Verb in Infinitive]

Step 1: Conjugate "Ir" (To go)

You must change the verb "Ir" depending on who is doing the action:

  • Yo: voy (I am going...)
  • Tú: vas (You are going...)
  • Él / Ella: va (He / She is going...)
  • Nosotros: vamos (We are going...)
  • Ellos: van (They are going...)

Step 2: Add "a" and the Action

  • Yo voy a comer (I am going to eat)
  • Tú vas a dormir (You are going to sleep)
