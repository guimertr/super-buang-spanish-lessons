Lesson 6: Verb Conjugations

In English, verbs don't change much. You say "I eat, you eat, we eat", and you only add an 's' for "he eats". In Spanish, every person has a different verb ending depending on when the action happens.

Conjugation is the act of modifying a verb to tell us WHO did the action, and WHEN it happened.

In our Interactive Explorer, you will be able to select any of our 11 core verbs and see exactly how they change for:

  • Presente: Actions happening today or every day.
  • Pasado: Actions that happened yesterday or in the past.
  • Futuro: Actions that will happen tomorrow or later.
