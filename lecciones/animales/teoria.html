Lesson 2: Animals & Gender

In Spanish, all nouns (including animals) have a gender. They are either Masculine or Feminine.

The Golden Rule:
If the animal is masculine, we use El (The).
If the animal is feminine, we use La (The).

Common Animals:

  • El Perro: The dog (Masculine)
  • El Gato: The cat (Masculine)
  • La Vaca: The cow (Feminine)
  • La Rana: The frog (Feminine)
  • El Mono: The monkey (Masculine)
